Inside the app

Scan a barcode or label

Scan the barcode for a product lookup, or take a clear photo of the ingredient list. See possible FODMAP considerations and what remains uncertain. Ingredient scanning is free.

Set your own tolerances

Record what you personally tolerate, including a small-portion setting. Your preferences personalise the advice; they do not change the ingredient analysis or establish a safe serving size.

📖

Keep a simple food diary

Record what you eat and how your gut feels. Small, regular entries can give you something more concrete to look back on than memory alone.

Guided Box, 4-7-8 and 5:5 breathing exercises offer a moment to pause. They are an optional relaxation tool, not a treatment for IBS.

Frequently asked questions

Good question.

What if the scanner cannot recognise my product?

Make sure the entire ingredient list is sharp and readable in good light. If it still does not work, you can add the product manually. Always check the original packaging.

Why is a product flagged as unsuitable?

BellyBliss identifies possible FODMAP considerations from the ingredient list and your personal tolerance settings. People respond differently to ingredients. A flag is not proof that a product will cause symptoms, and a favourable result is not a safety guarantee.

Can I use BellyBliss to make medical decisions?

No. BellyBliss offers observations based on your records and does not replace medical advice. Speak to a doctor or dietitian about health concerns. Do not use the scanner to check food allergies.

What happens to my data?

Your diary, tolerances and symptoms stay in the app on your phone. A barcode lookup sends the barcode and technical connection data to Open Food Facts. Subscriptions, notifications and security use external services. The privacy policy explains these distinctions.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel Premium through the store where you bought it. You can cancel at any time and retain access until the paid period ends. An Apple purchase and a Google Play purchase do not transfer between stores.
