BellyBliss privacy policy

Policy last updated: 21 October 2026.

Privacy at BellyBliss

This notice describes BellyBliss for iOS 1.6.1 (6) and Android 1.0 (6). BellyBliss processes data for your diary, product information, subscriptions, notifications and technical security. Not all processing takes place solely on your phone.

Your diary and personal settings

Food records, gut feelings, personal tolerances and the first name you enter are stored by the app on your device. The reviewed app code does not send this diary content to Firebase, RevenueCat, OneSignal or Open Food Facts. Local app data may be included in an Apple or Google device backup you have enabled. This is separate from a BellyBliss account or synchronisation between iOS and Android.

Camera, photos and product scans

The camera or a photo you select is used to read a barcode or ingredient list. Text recognition and ingredient assessment run locally in this release, using Apple Vision on iOS and Google ML Kit on Android. This release does not send photos or ingredients to an external analysis service. A barcode lookup sends the barcode and technical connection data, such as your IP address and app request information, to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. Barcode lookup therefore uses an internet connection.

Subscriptions and restoration

Apple or Google handles payment. RevenueCat helps the app verify purchases and restore Premium access. This involves an app user ID, purchase and subscription records, product IDs, transactions and technical app/device information. An automatically generated ID is a pseudonym; it does not mean that no personal data is processed. BellyBliss does not receive your full payment card number. Apple and Google Play purchases remain separate in this release. You cannot restore an Apple purchase on Android or a Google Play purchase on iOS.

Professional access and security

Firebase provides technical authentication, professional-access checks and processing of an access code if you enter one. This may include an automatically generated Firebase user ID, access status and an expiry date. App Check and platform security process technical information to check app requests. Server requests may generate technical logs including IP addresses, timestamps and errors. This does not create a new personal user account.

Notifications and technical usage data

OneSignal is initialised when the app starts. To provide notifications and operate the service, it processes installation/user identifiers, device/app information, session information and notification interactions. A push token is used when you allow push notifications. The SDK may also process purchase metadata. Denying notification permission does not automatically stop all technical SDK processing. You can turn off notifications in system settings. Android ML Kit also processes technical SDK diagnostics such as performance, errors and installation identifiers; the locally recognised photo is not sent to Google by text recognition.

Usage measurement in this release

Firebase Analytics is fully disabled in this iOS release; the Android app does not integrate Firebase Analytics. This new iOS version does not send scan, tolerance, diary, PDF or breathing events to Firebase Analytics. This does not disable necessary technical processing by subscription, notification and security services. Earlier iOS versions could send usage events, including applied tolerance categories. Updating does not automatically delete previously received analytics data.

Consultation overview and contact

A consultation PDF is generated locally from data recorded in the app. You choose whether and with whom to share it using your device’s sharing feature. The recipient or sharing app then processes the document under its own terms. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you include in your message. Only include information needed to address your question.

Service providers and processing off your device

BellyBliss uses Apple and Google for platform functions and payments, RevenueCat for subscriptions, Firebase/Google for authentication and security, OneSignal for notifications and Open Food Facts for product information. These organisations may process information on systems outside your country. Their privacy information and applicable contractual safeguards are relevant to that processing. Anthropic receives no scan content through this release.

Legal bases

We process the data necessary to provide the product information, access and subscription restoration you request to perform our agreement with you. We use necessary technical data for security and abuse prevention based on our legitimate interest in a reliable service. We handle privacy requests to comply with our legal obligations. These grounds do not provide general permission for additional usage measurement. You can withdraw notification permission in system settings; this does not automatically stop all SDK processing described above.

Retention periods

Your local diary and settings remain on your device until you delete them; you manage any device backups separately. No automatic expiry has been established for RevenueCat, OneSignal or separate Firestore profiles. Deletion of identifiable records is assessed and carried out on request where applicable. Expiry of an access right does not automatically delete its record.

The verified Cloud Logging settings are 30 days for ordinary logs and 400 days for required audit logs. Previously collected Firebase Analytics events are set to 2 months and user data to 14 months; new activity can restart the user retention period. These settings do not govern most aggregated standard reports. The new release stops the relevant Analytics collection but does not automatically erase old records.

Firebase states that authentication IP addresses are retained for a few weeks and other authentication data is removed from live systems and backups within 180 days after deletion is initiated. This is not a retention period for all Firebase services. Open Food Facts states 3 years for visitor logs containing IP addresses. Apple and Google retain their own payment and store records under their own policies; BellyBliss cannot independently erase those records.

Data deletion and other privacy requests

Email bellybliss.app@gmail.com with the subject ‘BellyBliss privacy request’. Tell us what you want to access, correct or delete and whether you use iOS or Android. Do not attach your diary, a full identity document or payment card details. If further information is needed to reliably locate your data, we ask only for what is necessary. An email address is not automatically linked to technical app identifiers.

Jean-Paul Terpstra registers and handles your request, normally within one month. If a legally permitted extension is necessary, we notify you within that first month and explain why. Where applicable, we arrange deletion of identifiable app, subscription, notification and technical profiles with the relevant providers. We explain what was deleted and what must remain because of a specific legal obligation, a valid exception or a technical backup period. We do not promise immediate deletion across every provider.

You manage local diary records and tolerances in the app and Apple or Google backups yourself. Data deletion does not cancel a subscription: cancel through the store where you purchased it. Using the app again may create necessary technical profiles again. We retain only the correspondence and minimum evidence needed to handle your request and any related complaint or obligation; unnecessary identity information is removed after verification. You may also request restriction or portability and object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Contact

Jean-Paul Terpstra is the data controller for BellyBliss, the Netherlands. Privacy questions and data requests: bellybliss.app@gmail.com. You may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority or the appropriate authority in your country.
