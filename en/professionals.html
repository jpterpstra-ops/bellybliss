Three tools. One conversation.

Ingredient scanning, a food and gut diary, and personal pattern views — practical information to discuss together.

Core feature

Ingredient scanning

Patients can scan a barcode or photograph the ingredient list. BellyBliss highlights possible FODMAP considerations and uncertainty, using the recognised text and personal tolerance settings. A scan cannot measure FODMAP content or establish a safe serving.

Local ingredient analysis. Product lookups, subscriptions, notifications and security use external services that process technical data. See the privacy policy.

Result

Reading a scan result

Recognised concerns and uncertain ingredients remain visible. Personal tolerance settings affect the advice, but do not make an unknown ingredient suitable. Always compare the result with the packaging.

Monitoring

Food and gut diary

Patients record food and how their gut feels over time. These entries provide something concrete to discuss with a doctor or dietitian, without relying entirely on memory.

Insights

Personal patterns

With Premium, patients can review possible associations between food entries and gut feelings. A pattern is a starting point for discussion, not proof of a cause.

What BellyBliss does — and where it stops

Clear boundaries for an informed recommendation.

✅ What BellyBliss does

Highlight possible FODMAP considerations from ingredient lists and personal settings.

Record food and gut feelings over time.

Show patterns as a discussion aid.

Keep the diary and personal settings locally on the device.

❌ What it does not do

Diagnose a condition or prescribe treatment.

Automatically upload the diary; external services do process technical data.

Replace a doctor or dietitian, or verify food allergy safety.

Guarantee complete product data or establish a safe FODMAP serving.
